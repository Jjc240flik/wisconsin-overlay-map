## GROUND RULES (from the buyer-demand call workflow — non-negotiable)

1. **Lead with the opportunity, not the price.** Never ask "what would you pay per lot?" early — it invites "where is it?" and undercuts the paper-flip framing. Let THEM name a number when ready.
2. **Location framing:** the site WAS publicly listed (LoopNet #22282309, off-market since Jun 2026), so "NE Rochester, off Broadway Ave" is fine. Do NOT share Ken's basis, the ownership structure, or your kicker position.
3. **The entitlement is the story:** 2003 GDP #193 approved 140–150 townhome units on R-2/R-3. Prefaced as "we've done the entitlement work so you don't deal with the city" — but NEVER claim it's fully marketable until GDP #193 status is reverified with the City of Rochester.
4. **Known objection to surface ourselves (rock):** the shale/rock was why it didn't sell at $1.2M. Name it on the first real call — get their reaction to the issue, not the price.
5. **No joint venture unless they bring it up.** Lead with sale. If they ask about partner structures, that's a separate conversation (see JV tab).
6. **Capture the decision-maker** — get a NAME + direct line, not a gatekeeper.
7. **Log everything verbatim** after each call: `after_contact_buyer_brief.md` (structured) + `full_raw_content.md` (verbatim source of truth). Update after EVERY contact, push to GitHub.

## THE QUESTIONS

### Step 1 — OPEN (pick one)
- "I'm Jason Christiansen — I represent an approved 23-acre townhome site in NE Rochester. Got three minutes to see if it's worth a longer conversation?"
- "You're one of the few builders actually doing attached product in Rochester — wanted to see if you're hungry for approved ground."

### Step 2 — CURRENT STATE (pick 2–3)
1. "How's your year going — how many units on track this year?"
2. "Where are your active Rochester communities right now?"
3. "Are you buying finished lots from developers, or buying raw/approved ground and building it yourself?"
4. "When you look at NE Rochester specifically — 55906 — what's your read on absorption for townhomes?"
5. "What's your current land inventory situation — set for a while, or actively looking?"

### Step 3 — THE PITCH (the story — say this, don't read it)
> "I've got a 23-acre approved townhome site in NE Rochester — off Broadway Avenue. It's zoned R-2/R-3, has a 2003 township approval for 140–150 attached units, utilities context done, and it's off-market right now. We've done the entitlement legwork so you're not sitting in front of the city for two years. I'm looking for the right builder who wants to take it from approved to built."

### Step 4 — DEMAND + STRUCTURE TEST (the money questions)
6. **The appetite question:**
   - "Is a 23-acre approved townhome site in NE Rochester something you'd actively pursue?"
   - "We built the plan around 140–150 units — does that size fit how you phase projects?"
7. **The product question:**
   - "Would you build this as for-sale townhomes, rentals, or a mix?"
   - "What's your target price point / size range if you were building on this site?"  *(they name the retail first — this is free market research)*
8. **The structure question:**
   - "Do you buy ground outright, or through takedown / option agreements?"
   - "Would a simultaneous close work for you — us selling the approved site to you, you closing with your construction line in place?"
9. **The rock question (raise it ourselves):**
   - "One thing we want to be straight about: this site has known shale/rock — it's why the prior listing at 1.2M sat. How do you underwrite rock? Would you want a geotech report before making an offer, or is that just part of your normal site work?"
   - "If we provided a geotechnical study in the package, does that change how quickly you could underwrite it?"
10. **The entitlement-reverification question (honest):**
    - "The 2003 GDP #193 approval is the backbone of the plan — have you ever worked with that type of approval in Rochester? What would you want to confirm before relying on it?"

### Step 5 — CLOSE
- "If everything checks out, could you come back to me first before anyone else on it?"
- "Who's the right person on your team to keep this moving — can I get their name and direct line?"
- "If I email you the one-page site summary, what's the best address?"
