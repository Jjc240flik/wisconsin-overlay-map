## GAPS / HONEST NOTES
1. **Land appetite unverifiable** for most names — contacts/roles are public-record sourced (news, LinkedIn, corporate sites); nobody was called. Rochester land prices paid in 2025–26 (Pulte $3.55M; Lennar $15.74M/367 ac ≈ $43K/ac; Millrose $84M/13 parcels) suggest institutional interest is real, but current underwriting (rates, DMC timelines) is unknown.
2. **Paper-flip reality check:** Lennar, Pulte, DRH all buy raw land and self-entitle (Lennar literally said "we're developing it ourselves"); a 2003 GDP approval that has never been built may need re-verification/re-entitlement (entitlements expire; 2003 GDP #193 status with City of Rochester should be confirmed before marketing). Buyers may discount the "entitled" premium.
3. **Apartment developers (Sherman, Roers, Enclave, Dominium)** are the most capital-active in Rochester right now (several $100M+ closes in 2025–26) but prefer downtown/urban or LIHTC product; a 23-acre NE-side site would need a rental-townhome or workforce-housing pitch to hit their screens.
4. **55+/senior angle** is hot (Del Webb 317 units, Trailside senior apartments) — the R-3 acreage could support age-restricted attached product; Del Webb (Pulte) is the obvious first call.
5. Local builders (Bigelow, R. Fleming, Elcor) have the product fit but typically buy finished lots, not 23-acre raw/paper parcels — price and lot-layout economics must support a builder-buyer model.
6. Contacts marked with phone/email come from public listings (MHP, BBB, company sites, news) and may have changed; verify before outreach.
