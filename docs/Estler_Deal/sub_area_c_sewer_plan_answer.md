## LIFT STATION LOCATION — CONFIRMED (village's own plan)
- **"The Sanitary & Water System Plan indicated a sanitary lift station in the northern part of Sub Area C."**
- **Lift Station No. 4** (Cedar Corp. Dec 2021 Master Plan §4.2.4.3): "will provide service for residential customers located within planning section 2A-1 and **will be located at the northeast corner of the section along Mayflower Drive**" — 30-year design population **1,032 people**, pumping ~**80,000 gpd**.
- Discharge: existing manhole near **Everglade Rd & Greenwood Rd** → 15" sewer → **Lift Station No. 1** (Everglade). "The discharge gravity sewer is 15" piping and is expected to have sufficient capacity... without requiring an upsize."
- **Lift Station No. 5**: Mayflower Dr north of railroad crossing, 150 people, 34,000 gpd, discharge to 10" main at Mayflower/Neubert.
- **Alternative discharge note:** "An alternative discharge location can be considered in the future which eliminates the need for double pumping; however the length of force main to achieve this may not be cost-effective."

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR ESTLER
1. **The lift station is the village's planned area infrastructure** (Lift Station No. 4, 1,032 residents of Section 2A-1 = 320 acres) — NOT just our 44-lot subdivision's pump.
2. **BUT the village's documented position is "development needs to financially support"** — so they expect the developer (us) to pay.
3. **The negotiation isn't "do we pay" — it's "how much and how shared":**
   - Our lift station is sized for the WHOLE area (1,032 people) — we're building capacity for future developers who should reimburse us
   - Doug Gunderson's adjacent project needs the same station — joint/shared cost is logical
   - The village's own master plan (their document) plans this station — they have a CIP-eligibility argument, even if their default answer is "developer pays"
4. **Cost benchmark:** small-mid station (our share ~$250K shared / $200–550K unshared) is realistic; the FULL station (1,032-pop area scale) would be $500K–$1.5M — which is exactly why the sharing/reimbursement structure matters.

## THE CRITICAL IMPLICATION FOR OUR NUMBERS

**The FULL area-scale Lift Station No. 4 is a $2.0–2.1M facility** — NOT the $200–500K we had in our estimates. Our $250K-shared / $500K-unshared numbers assumed a SMALL subdivision pump station, not the village's planned area station.

**But here's the nuance that matters:**
1. The $2.0–2.1M is the FULL station for the WHOLE 320-acre Sub-Area C (1,032 residents). Our 44-lot subdivision is ONE part of that service area.
2. The station will be built once and serve everyone — **the first developer to plat likely builds it, then later developers reimburse** (or the village CIPs it and developers pay connection fees).
3. **Doug Gunderson's adjacent Whispering Winds would share it** — his project needs the same station.
4. **Our realistic share is NOT $2M.** It's:
   - Our proportional share as first mover (could be $500K–$1M if we fund the whole station and get reimbursed), OR
   - A negotiated joint build (Doug + us + maybe village), OR
   - Village CIP + connection fees (if we can push the "area infrastructure" argument)
5. **This is a $250K–$1M+ swing in our profit model** — THE single biggest unknown cost item. It MUST be resolved before concept-plan spend.
