# LIFT STATION COST BENCHMARKS — Wisconsin/Midwest Real Project Data
Compiled: 2026-08-20 | VERIFIED numbers from primary sources; UNVERIFIED marked clearly. Labels: CONFIRMED / LIKELY / NO RELIABLE DATA.

## WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ESTLER
Our 44-lot subdivision in Greenville WI may need a lift station on the parcel's low ground (joint with adjacent developer Doug Gunderson's Whispering Winds). Internal estimate: $200–500K unshared, ~$250K shared. These benchmarks validate/correct that.

## VERIFIED COST DATA (primary sources)

### 1. Town of Waterford SD No. 1, Racine Co. WI — BEST DATASET (all $ REAL)
Source: Ruekert & Mielke study (Jan 2024), cost estimate (Oct 2024), bid results (Feb 2025), award + funding letter.
- Study Table 4 (Jan 2024): 20-station program total **$7,825,000**; small stations: **LS1 $143K, LS2 $175K**; large: LS6 $1,541K, LS10 $1,518K, LS15 (largest) $2,402K; SCADA $1,316K
- Oct 2024 line-item estimate: **small residential stations (LS1–LS4, 6–10 Hp) $106,600–$191,200**; LS15 with new building + 80 LF force main @ **$400/LF** = $1,466,300; program total w/ OH+contingency+eng **$8,538,175**
- **Bid (opened 2/13/25): low $5,979,145 (Lee Mechanical)**; awarded at that price. Funding: **WDNR Clean Water Fund loan $7,475,000 @ 3.90%/20-yr**
- **KEY PRECEDENT:** District community letter states all sewer extensions were added **"by developers or property owners at no cost to the District"** — developer-paid infrastructure is the norm

## BENCHMARK SUMMARY (what it means for Estler)

| Size | Typical total installed | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Small residential (5–50 homes, 6–10 Hp)** | **$100K–$200K** | Waterford LS1–LS4 $106,600–$191,200 |
| **Mid (50–150 homes)** | **$250K–$550K** | Wausau Crocker $530K; Waterford LS1/LS2 $143–175K |
| **Large (150+ homes / area-scale)** | **$500K–$1.5M+** | Wausau Cherry $825K; Cummins-Wagner $500K–$1M; Waterford LS6/LS10 $1.5M+ |
| Force main | **$100–$400/LF** | Waterford LS15 80 LF @ $400/LF |
| Generator / upgrades | **$600K+** | Waterloo NE |
| **Our 44-lot case (shared w/ Gunderson)** | **~$250K plausible** | Small-mid station, shared |
| **Our case (unshared)** | **$200K–$550K** | Small-mid station, full cost |

**Verdict on our estimate:** The **$200–500K unshared / ~$250K shared** range is REALISTIC — at the small-to-mid end for a 44-lot subdivision with a shared station. If the village treats it as AREA infrastructure (Lift Station No. 4, 1,032 residents), the cost could be higher ($500K–$1.5M) — which strengthens the argument for village participation.
