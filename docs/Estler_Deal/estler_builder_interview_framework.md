# CODY-METHOD BUILDER/DEVELOPER INTERVIEW FRAMEWORK — ESTLER 48-54 LOT SUBDIVISION
## The Module 1 demand-validation script — use with every contact from the buyer lists

## PURPOSE
Validate real, named demand for ~48-54 approved single-family lots in Greenville WI
(Sub-Area C, 0.5-1.0 ac lots) at $50-75K/lot (approval-stage exit), and identify
who would actually buy/take down the subdivision.

## THE TWO-PRICE CONTEXT (what we're really asking)
- We control 63.4 ac, N Mayflower Dr, Greenville — entitling to ~48-54 lots
- Our exit: sell APPROVED (pre-horizontal) lots at $50-75K each via simultaneous close
- Builder/developer then does horizontals OR buys finished lots at $80-140K
- We're NOT asking them to build now — we're testing whether the approved lot product
  at that price has a market

---

## INTERVIEW SCRIPT (5-10 min, phone)

### 1. OPEN (15 sec)
"Hi [NAME], this is Jason Christiansen. I'm working on a 50-lot subdivision in Greenville
and I'm talking to the builders who actually build there before I go any further.
Got 5 minutes?"

### 2. CURRENT STATE (1 min) — confirm they're real and active
- "How's your year going? How many homes are you on track to build this year?"
- "Where are your active communities right now?" (listen for Greenville/Appleton/Fox Valley)
- "Are you buying lots from developers, or do you build on your own land?"

### 3. THE DEMAND QUESTION (2 min) — the core test
- "If I could put 50 approved building lots in Greenville — 1-acre-ish, sewer and water
  planned to the site — would that be something you'd be interested in?"
- "What's your current lot inventory situation? Are you hungry for lots or set for a while?"
- "What's the most you've paid per lot recently, and what does a lot need to have
  (sewer, water, road, easements) for you to write that check?"

### 4. THE PRICE TEST (2 min) — Cody's backward math
- "If lots were fully approved and I could sell them at [50/60/75]K — before horizontal
  work — does that work in your numbers? What would you pay for an approved lot
  vs a finished lot?"
- "How many lots would you take? One, five, ten, or the whole phase?"
- "Do you typically buy lots outright, or through a takedown agreement where you
  commit as they come available?"

### 5. THE STRUCTURE TEST (1 min)
- "If I had it approved and wanted to do a simultaneous close — you pay me, I pay the
  seller, the same day — is that a structure you've done before? Comfortable with it?"
- "Who's the right person on your team for lot purchases?" (get a name + direct line)

### 6. CLOSE (30 sec)
- "Thanks — I'm putting the pieces together on the approval now. If it works,
  would you want me to come back to you first?"
- Get: name, title, direct phone, email, preferred lot count, price point.

## WHAT WE'RE LISTENING FOR (the verdict inputs)

| Signal | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|
| Lot appetite | "We're out of lots / need 10+" | "We're set for 2 years" |
| Price acceptance | $50-75K approved sounds fair | "I'd pay $30K" or "finished only" |
| Takedown structure | "Sure, done it before" | "I only buy finished lots from the city" |
| Greenville comfort | Builds there or nearby | "Too far from my base" |
| Volume | 30+ homes/yr | Custom 4-6 homes/yr (not our buyer) |
