**Allocation math (from Mark's own 2021 figures — 320 ac / 1,032 residents ≈ 413 units):**
- Per **unit**: 44 ÷ 413 × $2.0M = **~$213K** → ~$4,800/lot ✅
- Per **acre**: 63.4 ÷ 320 × $2.0M = **~$396K** → ~$9,000/lot ⚠️
- Full freight: **$2.1M** → −$929K ❌

**Why basis matters:** our site is low-density (44 lots on 63.4 ac = 0.69 lots/ac). The rest of the 320-acre service area will almost certainly plat denser, so a **per-acre** charge over-charges us relative to the units we actually add. **Per-unit is the basis to fight for.**

## ⚠️ THE CATCH NOBODY HAS SAID OUT LOUD

Recapture fixes the **P&L**, not the **cash**. Someone still has to front $2.0–2.3M and wait to be reimbursed at permit time as neighbors develop. "Our share is only $213K" is the *economic* burden; the *cash* burden is the whole station.

**The three questions that actually decide GO/KILL are therefore about the service area and first-mover protection — not about the station price.**
