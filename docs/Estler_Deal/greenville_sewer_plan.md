## 1. Nearest gravity sewer main & planned route — CONFIRMED
Source: *Wastewater System Master Plan / "Sewer Development Plan," Cedar Corp., Dec 2021 (final set 1-4-2022)*:
https://cms3.revize.com/revize/greenvillewi/R%20-%20Sewer%20Development%20Plan_Dec2021_Final%20set%20(1-4-2022).pdf
- Existing system: 3 lift stations — Joan Street, **Lift Station No. 1 ("Everglade Lift Station," NW side of Municipal Dr & Everglade Rd intersection)**, Lift Station No. 2 ("Meadow"). Wastes go via 42" interceptor to the **Fox West Regional WWTF**.
- **Nearest gravity mains to Mayflower/Everglade (Sub-Area C):**
  - **15" gravity sewer** at manhole near **Everglade Rd & Greenwood Rd**, draining to Everglade Lift Station No. 1 (§4.2.4.3, p.19)
  - **10" gravity sewer** at **Mayflower Dr & Neubert Rd**, flowing by gravity to the Village metering station (§4.2.4.4, p.20)
  - **8" main** at Mayflower Dr/Edgewood Dr area; Section 2 (Sub-Area C) tie-in planned at **MANHOLE E11634**, future 8" service (§4.3.3, Table 4-3)
- **Planned route for Sub-Area C (Section 2, 320 ac, west of Mayflower Dr, north of Everglade Rd):** "Depending on final development design, gravity flow from the southwestern portion of Section 2-A-1 to the existing collection system may be possible. **Any users located north of the Mayflower Drive/Edgewood Drive intersection will most likely need to be pumped by a lift station.**" (§4.3.3)
- The "diagonal route" phrase from PC minutes NOT found in any retrievable document — NO RELIABLE DATA on that label.
- Related trunk work: STH 76 & Hillview Rd Sanitary Sewer & Water Main project (12" upsizing Everglade Rd–Hillview Rd; Hillview Rd mains for Hillview Estates).

## 2. Lift station: village plan vs developer obligation — CONFIRMED (plan)/NO RELIABLE DATA (funding)
- **CONFIRMED:** The village's own adopted master plan plans **Lift Station No. 4** for Sub-Area C ("located at the northeast corner of the section along Mayflower Drive," 1,032 residents, ~80,000 gpd; discharge via 15" main to Lift Station No. 1) and **Lift Station No. 5** for the Mayflower/Neubert area (150 people, ~34,000 gpd; discharge to 10" main at Mayflower Dr/Neubert Rd) (§4.2.4.3, §4.2.4.4).
- **NO RELIABLE DATA:** whether the village or developer pays. Master plan Ch. 5/§4.4 (p. 53–54) contains **Lift Station Cost Estimates** but no funding source (CIP vs developer exaction vs TIF). The 2025 PC discussions (4/28, 5/19, 6/23) addressing this could not be retrieved.
- Hydraulic capacity upgrades needed: 8" sewer upstream of Lift Station No. 1 requires upgrade to min 12" to accept Lift Station No. 3 flows; Section 2/4 tie-ins "upstream of the lift station … expected to require a hydraulic capacity upgrade" (§4.2.4.2, §4.4.5). Additional capital beyond the lift station itself.

## KEY ANSWER FOR THE ESTLER DEAL
**The lift station is IN THE VILLAGE'S OWN ADOPTED MASTER PLAN (Lift Station No. 4), planned for our exact area (Sub-Area C, "northeast corner of the section along Mayflower Drive" — i.e., our parcel's low ground).** The village has been planning this since Dec 2021. Whether they pay or we pay is NOT yet established — that's the 2025 PC minutes question. But the fact that it's THEIR planned infrastructure (sized for 1,032 residents of the whole 320-ac area, not just our 44 lots) strengthens our position: this is area infrastructure, not a subdivision improvement. Cost-sharing / village CIP participation / reimbursement from later developers are all on the table.
