## 4. TYPICAL PURCHASE / DEVELOPMENT STRUCTURE — EVIDENCE (developer agreements)

The Greenville standard (all CONFIRMED from official resolutions/agreements):
1. **Developer buys raw land outright** (option/earnest structures are private; no recorded option found in county records for any Greenville plat — NO RELIABLE DATA on option terms; the observable pattern is outright acquisition then entitlement).
2. **Plat approval conditioned on a Developer's Agreement** requiring all public improvements at the developer's sole expense, secured by a **financial guarantee (LOC/escrow ~120% of engineer's estimate)** with a completion deadline:
   - Savannah Heights Ph I (Res 14-18, 7/9/18): $320,803.42 guarantee, 120% LOC/escrow, 2-yr completion.
   - Fox Highlands Condos (Res 63-20, 8/24/20): LOC $188,709.72 per Exhibit 2 cost estimate.
   - Jennerjohn Estates (Res 15-20 + 48-22): all improvements at sole expense; 3-yr extension granted (2022) with final plat deadline 9/26/2025.
   - Country Meadows (Res 54-23): final plat with 3 conditions on the developer's agreement.
3. **Developer builds horizontal, then sells lots** — to builders in bulk (VW/Keystone/Virtue in SH6) or retails via broker (N.J. Schmidt, Virtue, Cypress listings; North Appleton closings through First American Title).
4. **JV/option structures: NO RELIABLE DATA** — no JV or option developer agreements found in any Greenville public record; the model is land-ownership + LOC-backed development agreements. For the Estler exit, the realistic structure is **outright simultaneous close at plat approval** (matches how FDG exited Fox Highlands and how Dercks DeWitt buys land), with the buyer assuming the developer-agreement obligations (or novating the LOC to them — the agreement is assignable with Village consent, as Res 9-26 shows for Fox Highlands' new developer).
