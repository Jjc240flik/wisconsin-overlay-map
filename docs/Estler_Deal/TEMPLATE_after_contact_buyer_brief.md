# AFTER-CONTACT BUYER BRIEF — [PROPERTY NAME] ([acres] ac, [location])
**Purpose:** Structured feedback log for all post-call / post-text / post-screenshot communications from builder & developer outreach ([Property] deal, Module 1 buyer validation).
**Rule:** Structured findings live here. **Full verbatim transcripts/texts/screenshots live in the sibling file `full_raw_content.md`** — every transcript is preserved IN ITS ENTIRETY, no summarizing.

## HOW TO ADD A NEW ENTRY
Append a new row-block with: **DATE | COMPANY | CONTACT # | CONTACT NAME | FINDING | IMPACT | OTHER VALUABLE INFO**.
Paste the FULL verbatim content (transcript/text/screenshot) into `full_raw_content.md` under a matching ENTRY header.
